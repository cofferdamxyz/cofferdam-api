Each finished turn is metered per account and month in `ChatUsageDO`, and per month and model beside it with the router left out (`chatUsageStore.ts` holds the SQL, run on `node:sqlite` in `test/chatUsage.test.ts`; `chatUsageDO.ts`; priced from the neurons the model reports, else `MODEL_PRICES` in `services/inference`; the router from Clef's reported tokens, else its state's characters over four; model rows with no turns, the Work routes' document calls, stay out of the cost of a turn in `capEstimate`) through `waitUntil`; `CHAT_MONTHLY_USD_CAP` (dollars of estimated cost, not tokens) refuses an account with `429 fair_use_cap` and `AI_MONTHLY_BUDGET_USD` (the plane-wide row `plane:global`, which every hosted AI op must also record to) refuses everyone with `503 ai_budget_reached`, both before any model call, and `GET /v1/chat/usage` answers the month, its `models` most-used first and what is left of the cap in dollars and estimated turns, behind the same gate.
- `xmtp/` is the XMTP operator (workspace decision 0042), three Workers that deploy on their own and are not the plane: `backend/` (the XMTP 8 backend container, `backend.toml`, ingress slot `INGRESS=off` until the inbound-TCP beta), `push-relay/` (decision 0040: sealed tokens, APNs alert or FCM data, no storage, no `console`, observability off; `test/xmtp/pushRelay.test.ts` enforces it) and `cleanup/` (hourly expired-envelope deletion; its retention vars mirror `backend.toml` `[retention]`, checked by `test/xmtp/config.test.ts`; `src/health.ts` checks the backend's schema first, stores it under the KV key `health`, skips the run until the tables exist, and answers `GET /health`, reachable only through `wrangler dev --remote`).
