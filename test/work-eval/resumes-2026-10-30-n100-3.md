# Real-resume benchmark, 2026-10-30

Generated by `test/workResume.eval.test.ts` (RESUME_EVAL=1; workspace decision 0021, the founder's exception of 2026-10-28) on 100 resumes of Kaggle's public LiveCareer dataset, seed 1, outside every repository; 63 of 2484 resumes carry an email or a phone number and are never sampled. Pages as the phone sends them (JPEG, long side 1,600 px). The cloud reader gets the PDF's text layer instead of the pages, as it would read a CV whose identity the phone stripped first. Each field is scored against the labels in the resume's own HTML: right (counting those the worker would also be asked about), or wrong, and a wrong field nobody would ask about is silent. Readings made with the parse prompt tagged `b269702c`. Months: a job's length, a current job running to the resume's latest date, right within one month. Certificates: the keywords the certifications section names (OSHA, CDL, forklift, …). Counts only; no resume's text, title or date is in this file. 5 min in all.

## The identity strip

Each resume was given a made-up identity (`test/work-eval/stripBench.ts`, version 1): a name in one of five spellings, an email, a US phone and, on 69 of 100, a birth date in one of six formats, at the top, the end or in the middle (40 top, 31 end, 29 middle), the name repeated in a closing line on 35. The account was not bound: only the emails were stripped before the text went to the cloud reader.

- Still readable in the text sent: names 100/100, birth dates 69/69, emails 0/100, phones 100/100 (phones are not stripped).
- Removed and not the person's: 0 spans, 0 characters.
- The reader's identity, on the 100 resumes read: the name 97 the person's, 0 a placeholder, 1 none, 2 another; the phone 98 the person's; the email 98 a placeholder, 2 none.
- The email the phone would show, the first the strip removed: 100/100 the person's.

Cells: the share right, and in brackets the share wrong with no question to the worker. Questions per resume: the fields a resume would still ask its worker about.
