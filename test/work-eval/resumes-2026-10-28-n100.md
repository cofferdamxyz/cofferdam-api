# Real-resume benchmark, 2026-10-28

Generated by `test/workResume.eval.test.ts` (RESUME_EVAL=1; workspace decision 0021, the founder's exception of 2026-10-28) on 100 resumes of Kaggle's public LiveCareer dataset, seed 1, outside every repository; 63 of 2484 resumes carry an email or a phone number and are never sampled. Pages as the phone sends them (JPEG, long side 1,600 px). Each field is scored against the labels in the resume's own HTML: right (counting those the worker would also be asked about), or wrong, and a wrong field nobody would ask about is silent. Months: a job's length, a current job running to the resume's latest date, right within one month. Certificates: the keywords the certifications section names (OSHA, CDL, forklift, …). Counts only; no resume's text, title or date is in this file. 9 min in all.
