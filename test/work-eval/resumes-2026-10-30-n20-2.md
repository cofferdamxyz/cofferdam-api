# Real-resume benchmark, 2026-10-30

Generated by `test/workResume.eval.test.ts` (RESUME_EVAL=1; workspace decision 0021, the founder's exception of 2026-10-28) on 20 resumes of Kaggle's public LiveCareer dataset, seed 1, outside every repository; 63 of 2484 resumes carry an email or a phone number and are never sampled. Pages as the phone sends them (JPEG, long side 1,600 px). The cloud reader gets the PDF's text layer instead of the pages, as it would read a CV whose identity the phone stripped first. Each field is scored against the labels in the resume's own HTML: right (counting those the worker would also be asked about), or wrong, and a wrong field nobody would ask about is silent. Readings made with the parse prompt tagged `b269702c`. Months: a job's length, a current job running to the resume's latest date, right within one month. Certificates: the keywords the certifications section names (OSHA, CDL, forklift, …). Counts only; no resume's text, title or date is in this file. 0 min in all.
